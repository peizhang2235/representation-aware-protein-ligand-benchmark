# V1.13 standalone decision

**Decision:** `NO_ELIGIBLE_CONFIRMATORY_COHORT`

The outcome-blind GtoPdb 2026.2 audit retained 92 exact-pair- and
source-document-disjoint ligand-target pairs across six eligible targets. The
locked standalone thresholds required at least 100 pairs and eight targets.
Structure parsing (99.82%), sequence resolution (100%), per-target support,
scaffold diversity, reference resolution, and source-concentration gates passed.

No affinity outcome was unblinded and no frozen-model prediction was generated.
The result is therefore a feasibility failure, not evidence for or against model
performance. The v1.13 thresholds must not be relaxed retrospectively, and this
package must not be described as an independent replication.

A separate, prospectively registered multi-source ordinal validation may reuse
the six frozen blind target series, provided it has its own estimand, feasibility
gates, decision branches, and immutable provenance. Such a study does not alter
the v1.13 decision.
