# Data attribution and change notice

BindingDB-derived material in this candidate was filtered, normalized,
deduplicated, aggregated, joined to frozen predictions, and summarized for the
reported analyses. These transformations are changes from the source records.

Data curated by BindingDB are described by the BindingDB resource article as
CC BY 4.0. Records imported from ChEMBL retain the ChEMBL CC BY-SA 3.0
Unported terms. The mixed temporal files are conservatively treated under the
share-alike source terms for their database-derived fields.

Required citations:

- Liu T, Hwang L, Burley SK, et al. BindingDB in 2024: a FAIR knowledgebase of
  protein-small molecule binding data. Nucleic Acids Res. 2025;53:D1633-D1644.
  doi:10.1093/nar/gkae1075.
- Zdrazil B, Felix E, Hunter F, et al. The ChEMBL Database in 2023: a drug
  discovery platform spanning multiple bioactivity data types and time periods.
  Nucleic Acids Res. 2024;52:D1180-D1192. doi:10.1093/nar/gkad1004.
